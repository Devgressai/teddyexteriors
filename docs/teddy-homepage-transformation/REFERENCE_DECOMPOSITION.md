# Reference Decomposition

Three home-services sites studied for principles — not patterns to copy. Fetched 2026-10-01.

- **GVD Renovations** — Northern California remodeler, strong proof stacking + aspirational imagery
- **Cobex Construction Group** — Sacramento roofing/siding/windows/solar, multi-step form + narrative case studies
- **US Quality Construction** — Kansas City exterior remodeler, review-platform density + James Hardie Elite positioning

---

## §1 — GVD Renovations

### What works

| Dimension | Observed move | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust density** | "4.9 / 921+ reviews" badge appears 3× down the page, plus 6-platform aggregation | Relentless reinforcement; different platforms agreeing = no cherry-picking |
| **Localism + scale** | "Thousands of Northern California homeowners" + specific client cities (Fair Oaks, Lincoln) | Big-company credibility balanced against neighbor-friendly trust |
| **One-stop narrative** | Repeated phrase "all under one roof" | De-risks the fear of juggling contractors |
| **Hero** | Full-bleed aspirational image + headline + two proof-first CTAs ("Watch Testimonial Videos" / "View Our Work") + 4.9★ badge | Proof-first CTA pair is unusual and strong; most contractors push straight to form |
| **Service cards** | 4-column grid, **photo-led** (no icons) — kitchen cabinetry, bath tile, siding profile, decking | Imagery alone differentiates; no need for differentiating icons |
| **Project presentation** | Horizontal carousel, each slide = project image + name + client + location | Single-item reveal forces editorial pacing |
| **Testimonials** | Carousel of individual cards with headshot + 50–150 word quote + platform badge | Avatar + platform logo makes quotes feel audited, not fabricated |

### What's weak

- **Hero overlay-on-image** with centered text is the exact pattern the mandate bans.
- **Repeated star-rating stat block** 3× feels desperate on close read.
- "Awards" callouts (BuildZoom top 1%, BBB A+) are easy to fake-looking.
- Service cards are **visually interchangeable** — same aspect, same chrome, same CTA label.
- Carousel-only project reveal is slow for returning visitors who want to scan.

### What to steal

1. **Photo-led service cards** (no icons) — Teddy already partly does this; commit harder.
2. **Multi-platform review aggregation** — Teddy has 6 JDI profiles; render them visually.
3. **Specific client cities inline** (vs generic "we serve the Bay Area") — Teddy has 11 real cities.
4. **Repeated proof** — but **vary the form** (one credential rail at top, one reviews band mid, one license-number callout at footer). Don't just repeat the same stat block.

## §2 — Cobex Construction Group

### What works

| Dimension | Observed move | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero** | Asymmetric — headline/subhead left, form right | Form is already in the hero; the ask is lower-friction |
| **Service selector** | "I WANT >" buttons immediately below hero (Roofing / Siding / Windows / Solar) | Intent capture before scrolling |
| **Project case studies** | Each project has customer name + location + testimonial + objectives + outcomes + 6–20 photos + team-member quote | Transforms portfolio from "look what we built" to "here's how we solved YOUR problem" |
| **Team bio cards** | Not just name + role — includes "favorite thing about your job" + "interesting fact" | Humanizes beyond job titles; converts credentials into character |
| **Multi-step form** | 5 sequential steps instead of one long form | Reduces cognitive load; completion rate goes up on home-services forms |
| **Google review carousel with company replies** | Shows both sides of the conversation | 2-way exchange = authentic, not editorialized |
| **Credential clustering** | 4 trust badges (Platinum, BBA, Quality, Angi) in one cluster | One strong block beats scattered badges |
| **Financing transparency** | "Zero Down Financing Available" mid-page | Addresses affordability objection without being transactional |

### What's weak

- **Service cards are text-only** — no imagery differentiation; weaker than GVD's photo-led approach.
- **17 sections** is too many; the page is a scrollathon.
- **Office locations grid of 7** feels franchise-y rather than regional.
- Hero form competes with the headline; neither wins.

### What to steal

1. **Project case-study depth** — Teddy's project detail template already supports this; make the homepage project feature deeper.
2. **Credential clustering** (one block, not scattered) — Teddy's CredentialRail does this; make it stronger.
3. **Review-with-reply pattern** — if Teddy has Google reviews with JDI's replies, pull them in.
4. **Progressive form disclosure** — Teddy's EstimateSection is single-step; consider splitting into 2 (service + location, then details).

### What NOT to steal

- The 5-step form is appropriate for Cobex's roofing-sales model; Teddy's exterior work is often more consultative. A 2-step form is enough.
- The 17-section scrollathon.
- The "I WANT >" service selector pattern feels transactional; Teddy's positioning is premium / consultative, so a photographic service exploration beats it.

## §3 — US Quality Construction

### What works

| Dimension | Observed move | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero** | Left-aligned text overlay + 4.9/201 reviews badge below CTA | Reviews count is honest ("201 reviews" > "921+") |
| **Review aggregation row** | 6 platforms in one scannable row | Proof density without clutter |
| **License transparency** | "License #LC001476684" + "$2M Workman's Comp & Liability Insurance" shown explicitly | Concrete legal backing — contractors who hide numbers feel sketchy |
| **"Why Us" dual-format** | List + logo grid (both representations) | Repetition in different forms outperforms repetition in same form |
| **SEO long-form block** | "Missouri & Kansas Decking & Siding Contractor" mid-page with 4–5 paragraphs | Local authority without feeling salesy |
| **Video testimonials link to project photos** | Each video card links to specific project | Social proof directly tied to portfolio |
| **Blog-to-service bridge** | "Mold and Mildew on Siding" blog post links back to siding service page | Education drives conversion |
| **Geographic link carpet** | 50+ city links in footer and service pages | SEO + accessibility for "[city] + siding contractor" searches |
| **James Hardie Elite + NARI badges** | Manufacturer-level certifications displayed | Buyers trust contractor-level certs; mentioning the specific tier is key |

### What's weak

- **Service cards still generic** (4 cards, same geometry, SVG icons).
- **Hero headline "Preferred Kansas City Exterior Remodeling Company"** — "Preferred" is weaker than "Trusted" or specific proof.
- **Blog section placement** at the end feels tacked on.
- **Geographic link carpet** can feel spammy — only works when it links to real local pages.

### What to steal

1. **Honest review count** — Teddy's parent entity has real review totals on each platform; show them when verified.
2. **License-number transparency** — Teddy already shows WA L&I + OR CCB; keep elevating.
3. **Dual-format proof** — list of principles + corresponding logo grid.
4. **Blog-to-service bridges** — Teddy's resource guides should always link back to the service page they inform.
5. **Local long-form block** — a mid-page "Siding and Exterior Work in Vancouver, Washington and the Portland Metro" paragraph section is both SEO-useful and feels authentic.

### What NOT to steal

- The 50+ city link carpet unless each city has a real page with real content.
- Manufacturer-tier badges we don't actually hold (James Hardie Elite / LP Master Level) until the owner confirms Teddy's program status.
- The "Senior & Veteran Project Discounts" bullet — we don't have a confirmed discount program.

## §4 — Teddy Reference Synthesis

Design principles **adopted** from the reference set, filtered through the mandate's "anti-generic" / "logo-removal" / "alternate-industry" tests.

### Adopted principle 1 — Photo-led service navigation
**Source:** GVD + US Quality
**Teddy interpretation:** Hero service (siding replacement) gets a 16:10 editorial image with real exterior project photography. Supporting services (windows, painting, trim/gutters, envelope, whole-exterior) each get their own smaller editorial image at **varied aspect ratios** (3:4 vertical, 1:1, 5:3 wide). No matching card chrome.
**Why not GVD's version:** GVD uses identical aspect ratios across its 4 service cards. The uniformity makes them feel interchangeable. Teddy varies ratio deliberately.
**Do NOT copy:** GVD's hover-reveal pattern or "Learn More" button label.

### Adopted principle 2 — Multi-platform review aggregation
**Source:** GVD, Cobex, US Quality (all three)
**Teddy interpretation:** A compact typographic row of 6 real review platforms linked to the JDI profiles. No fabricated aggregate rating displayed on-site; the links are the proof. If the owner confirms the real rating numbers at the moment of launch, those can be added.
**Why not GVD's version:** GVD displays "4.9 / 921+" across the whole page. Teddy's parent entity has real numbers but they belong to the JDI brand, not Teddy; we show the links and let the reader verify.
**Do NOT copy:** Rotating star-rating badges, "Reviews" counter widgets, or aggregated schema markup.

### Adopted principle 3 — Project case-study depth
**Source:** Cobex
**Teddy interpretation:** The homepage ProjectFeature leads to a case-study page that documents: location, services, materials, original condition, scope, products installed, dimensions, substrate findings, moisture details, change-order handling, outcome. The content model already supports all of this. The homepage shows **one** project with enough information to decide to click; the detail page delivers the depth.
**Do NOT copy:** Cobex's 20-photo galleries or "team member commentary overlay" pattern; those add noise.

### Adopted principle 4 — Credential clustering
**Source:** Cobex
**Teddy interpretation:** One strong CredentialRail block (WA L&I + OR CCB + warranty duration + territory). No scattered badges elsewhere; the License/Credentials page carries the detail.
**Do NOT copy:** 4-logo manufacturer badge clusters until Teddy's actual manufacturer program status is confirmed.

### Adopted principle 5 — Mid-page local-authority long-form
**Source:** US Quality
**Teddy interpretation:** A single mid-page editorial paragraph (not a card grid) that explains why Pacific Northwest exterior work requires specific attention — climate, moisture, flashing, seasonal sequencing. This is distinct from the EnvelopeDetail section (which is technical/visual); this is editorial authority. Serves both SEO and emotional positioning.
**Do NOT copy:** US Quality's keyword-stuffed version or 50-city link carpet.

### Adopted principle 6 — Dual-format proof
**Source:** US Quality
**Teddy interpretation:** The CredentialRail at the top uses typographic numbers. The "Why Teddy" section uses principles-as-paragraphs paired with real photography. Different forms of proof at different scroll depths.
**Do NOT copy:** Logo-grid redundancy within the same section.

### Adopted principle 7 — Rhythm variation
**Source:** None directly — this is a mandate rule
**Teddy interpretation:** Alternate section backgrounds between warm bone (default), pure white (featured content), and deep brand green (envelope + final CTA). Vary section density: dense editorial hero → compact proof row → open service composition → immersive project → technical envelope → human trust → regional authority → process → social → education → conversion → footer.
**Why:** The references all have breathable rhythm, but each has a repetitive section geometry. Teddy's rhythm comes from alternating image scale, type weight, and background tone.

### Principles explicitly REJECTED

- **Centered-headline-over-image hero** (GVD, US Quality) — banned by mandate §02.
- **Rotating star-rating widget** — fabrication risk and self-serving aggregateRating ban per master brief §12.
- **Hero form** (Cobex) — competes with headline; Teddy's hero is editorial, form lives in a dedicated section near the bottom.
- **"I WANT >" service selector buttons** (Cobex) — transactional feel; Teddy is consultative.
- **Six identical "Why Us" cards with icons** — banned by mandate; use paragraph+image proof narrative instead.
- **Four identical numbered process cards** — banned by mandate; use horizontal editorial timeline.
- **50-city footer link carpet** — spammy without matching pages.
- **Fake urgency** / countdown / "limited offer" — banned by mandate §14.
- **Autoplay carousels** — banned by mandate §14C.
